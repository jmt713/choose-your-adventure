Scene 2: 🔥 Defend your mountain and roar your name to the sky?

The moment you choose, the cave trembles—as if the mountain itself bows to your will. You step into the light, the world blazing gold on your scales. Wind howls around you, carrying the scent of iron and fire.

 Their weapons glint like scattered stars. Below, the villagers freeze - for a heartbeat, no one moves. Then one of them—a knight draped in blackened armor—raises a banner torn from your old home.

“The beast returns!”

Their arrows rise like a storm.

You inhale. The air vibrates in your chest, the ancient power coiling through your ribs. You remember your name—not the one they gave you, but the one carved in the bones of the world.

“I am Flameborn!”

Your roar splits the clouds. Trees bend. The sky answers. Fire erupts—not as destruction, but as truth. The flames dance around you, forming the shape of wings within wings, a storm of molten light. The arrows melt before they reach you.

Still, the knight stands his ground, shouting over the inferno:

“Then burn us, monster! Or prove you are still one of us!”

The words strike deeper than any blade. Beneath the fire, a flicker of memory—the faces of those you once loved—struggles against the fury rising within you.

The ancient voice returns, curling through your mind like smoke:

“Even dragons must choose their fire… vengeance or mercy.”
