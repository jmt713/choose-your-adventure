
Fire Breather
=========
Scene 1:
-------


You wake with fire in your lungs.

Not pain—power.

Heat pulses beneath your skin—or rather, your scales. Ash drifts through the mouth of the cave, glowing in the morning light. You stretch, talons scraping stone, wings unfurling for the first time.

You are not human. Not anymore.

You are dragon.

And you are not alone.

Far below, tiny figures gather at the foot of your mountain. Smoke curls from the village you once called home. They carry steel. They chant your name—not with honor, but with fear.

From the depths of the cave, a voice rises—ancient, thunderous, yours-but-not:

“You have three choices, young wyrm…”

Do you:

- [🕊 Fly away—leave the mountain behind and discover what you are before facing them?](./fly-away.md)

- [🔥 Defend your mountain and roar your name to the sky?](./dragon-defend.md)

- [🌫 Hide in the shadows and listen to what the humans say?](./dragon-hide-listen.md)
