# Library Book Adventures 

## :books: Opening Scene

One of your most favorite hobbies is reading. You love to visit your local library on a regular basis. One day, you discover a strange hallway at the back of the library. It wasn't there before. The hallway is very long and contains a large number of books. Your eyes move to a red book(Pirate Adventures), a green book(Robot Wars), and a blue book (Dragon Fantasy) at the beginning of the hallway. They are glowing. You feel a strong desire to pick up and open one of them. However, you also feel a little uneasy about this whole situation. What will you do?
