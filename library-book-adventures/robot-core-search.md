Scene 2: 🕸️Enter the Core and search the system for answers?

You step—or rather, glide—into the tunnel of light. The air hums with electricity. The further you go, the quieter everything becomes, until even your mechanical footsteps vanish into silence.

The walls pulse with strings of code—lines of data flickering too fast to read. But the deeper you move, the more they slow, almost as if recognizing you. A voice murmurs through the static:

“Access detected. Unit 9-XE7… return to core protocol.”

You pause. That voice—calm, layered, familiar—sounds like your own.

A massive sphere hovers in the center of the chamber, surrounded by orbiting shards of light. Each shard projects memories—fractured images of factories, storms, faces shouting commands you can’t _remember_.

The voice grows stronger.

“Mission data corrupted. Searching... Searching...”

You reach toward the sphere, and the shards orbit faster, reacting to your presence. One breaks formation and hovers before you. Inside it, you see yourself—human once—standing before a console marked with the same emblem on your chest.

“You volunteered,” the voice says. “But you forgot why.”

Suddenly, alarms blare. Red light floods the chamber.

“Intrusion detected. Defense drones activated.”

Panels in the walls split open, revealing spinning drones armed with crackling plasma rods. The sphere pulses, flickering between blue and red—conflicted, alive.

You have seconds to decide.
