🔥 Ending: Oath of Guardianship

You lower your head, not in defeat, but in reverence. The heat within your chest burns steady—no longer wild, but radiant, a hearthlight in place of a wildfire. Around you, the wind hushes, and the valley holds its breath.

"I have taken much," you begin, voice resonant with memory and power. "But I have learned more. I cannot undo the past—but I can forge the future."

The knight lowers his blade. The clatter of steel meeting soil is the first note in a new melody. Behind him, villagers step forward—hesitant, but not afraid.

"I offer you this," you continue, wings arching skyward, casting no shadow this time but light, refracted through rising embers. "Not tribute, not fear, but protection. I will guard this valley, this home, from whatever lies beyond the mountain’s spine. You need not worship me, nor fear me. Only remember—what we were, what we could be."

Silence, then the murmur of awe. A child dares approach, wide-eyed, her tiny hand outstretched. You lower a claw, gentle as snowfall, and she places a flower upon it—a symbol of trust, fragile and fierce.

The knight kneels. Not to a god, but to a guardian.

"Then let us remember together," he says.

And so, you take your place not as a tyrant above, but as a sentinel among. The sky becomes your watchtower, the mountain winds your messengers. In time, songs are sung not of the flame that once destroyed, but of the fire that now protects.

And when danger stirs beyond the peaks, the villagers no longer tremble in fear.

They look to the sky.

And they wait for you—the wyrm who remembers home.
