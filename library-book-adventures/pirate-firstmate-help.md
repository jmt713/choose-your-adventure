⚓ A Whisper Below Deck
===

 Scene 3: 
 ---

You wait for the moment the crew is busy—tying ropes, scanning the storm, muttering prayers. Then you lean toward the first mate, a sharp-eyed woman with a half-faded compass tattooed on her throat.

“Walk with me,” you mutter low, nodding toward the stairs below deck.

She doesn’t hesitate. That’s either loyalty… or curiosity.

Down in the lantern-lit passageway, wood creaks and the storm’s voice dulls. You stop beside a row of barrels and turn to her.

“Be honest. You know I’m not your Captain,” you say. “But I need your help pretending to be.”

She watches you for a moment, jaw clenched. Then she exhales slowly, almost a laugh.

“I knew the moment you called for a chart instead of the starboard scope. The real Captain would’ve barked a storm chant and tied the wheel down by now.”

You freeze.

“But,” she continues, stepping closer, “you carry the coat. And the ship responds. That… thing on the horizon—she only appears for the blood-bound. That means you.”

“The Isle of Echoes?”

She nods. “Legend says it reveals itself only to the one who carries the curse—and the choice.”

“Choice?”

“To remember who you were… or to become who the ship thinks you are.”

Suddenly, the ship lurches. The lantern swings violently. You feel a pulse in your palm—hot, sharp—like the book is waking up again inside you.

She meets your eyes. “Whatever’s coming, you’re in it now, Captain. But I’ll help you… if you make one promise.”

“What?”

“When the time comes, you don’t run from the Echo.”

Before you can respond, a deckhand shouts from above.

“Storm’s shifted! Something’s coming off the water!”

Do you:

⚔️ [Race back to the helm and take command of the crew?]

🌀 [Ask the first mate what she meant by ‘the ship responds’]
