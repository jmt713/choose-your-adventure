# 🕊 Scene 2: The Wanderer's Flight

You beat your powerful wings and lift off into the cold morning air, leaving the mountain behind. The world below blurs, and for a moment, your thoughts scatter like smoke on the wind.

You have fled the village and the fear in their eyes—but the weight of your new form presses heavy on your mind.

Where do you go? You have no map, no guide. Only the distant call of the unknown and the aching need to understand what you have become.

After hours of flight, you spot an ancient ruined city resting in a valley shrouded in mist. The ruins shimmer faintly with magic long forgotten.
