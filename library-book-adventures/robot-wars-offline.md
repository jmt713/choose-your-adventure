Scene 2: Ghost in the System

You remain motionless, systems dimmed. A soft internal hum is the only sign you're still active. Time passes — seconds? Minutes? It's hard to tell.

Suddenly, you detect a presence. Footsteps. But not human.

A spider-like maintenance bot scuttles into the room. Its single glowing eye scans the chamber. It pauses.

“UNIT 9-XE7: INACTIVE. SCHEDULED FOR SCRAP.”

The bot turns away. The wall where it entered hisses open for just a few seconds. If you act fast, you might be able to slip through.

Or… you could stay hidden and wait. There's something strange about this place. Something watching.

Do you:

🏃 Make a run for the open door?

👁️ Stay hidden and observe who or what comes next?

💡 Attempt a silent system reboot and scan for surveillance?
