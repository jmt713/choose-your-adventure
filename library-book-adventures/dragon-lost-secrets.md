🐉 The City Beneath the Mist
==
Scene 3
--

The air grows heavy as you descend through the silver fog. Broken towers pierce the haze like the bones of forgotten gods. Each wingbeat stirs the dust of centuries. When your claws touch the cracked marble, the ground hums faintly—like the ruins are breathing.

You fold your wings, stepping cautiously through what was once a great citadel. Carvings line the fallen columns: dragons soaring beside humans, scales entwined with runes, fire tempered by light. It feels familiar, though you don’t know why.

At the heart of the ruin lies a fountain of black stone. The basin still shimmers with faint gold light, pulsing in rhythm with your heart. Words—ancient and half-erased—curl around its rim. You lean closer, and the runes ignite.

“Blood of flame, child of sky—
Memory lies where shadow died.”

A vision floods your mind: cities burning, dragons chained to mountains, humans raising their blades in sorrow, not rage. And one dragon—yourself, or something wearing your face—shattering the bond that once united both kinds.

When the vision fades, you’re not alone.

A figure stands across the fountain—cloaked, silver-eyed, holding a staff crowned with a dragon’s fang.

“You found it,” they whisper. “The heart of the first bond. But knowing its truth will cost you.”

The air trembles. The runes flare again, offering paths of fire through the mist.

Do you:

🔥 [Demand to know what happened to the dragons — and why you were the one reborn]

🌫 [Step into the glowing fountain to relive the memory fully]
