# 💧 The Last Word

You take a slow, steady breath. The roaring fire within you dims—not extinguished, but tempered by something deeper.

The flames around you shrink, flickering like candlelight. Your wings fold, casting a long shadow over the valley below.

The knight’s eyes widen in disbelief as you descend, your voice steady and clear:  
_"I am Flameborn—both dragon and memory of those I once was."_

The villagers lower their weapons, whispers rising like tentative wind. The knight steps forward, his armor battered but his gaze searching.  
_"Then prove it,"_ he says softly, _"Show us the wyrm who remembers home."_

Your throat tightens. Faces flicker in your mind—the family, the friends, the love lost to fire and time.

The ancient voice murmurs:  
_"Mercy is no weakness, young wyrm, but a new kind of fire."_
