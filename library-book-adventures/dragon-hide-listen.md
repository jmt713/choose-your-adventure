🐉 Stone-Silent
==
Scene 2:
--

You shrink deeper into the shadows of your cave, nestling between stone outcroppings and old bones. Your ember-warm breath fades to stillness. Even the wind seems to hush around your coils.

Below, the humans begin to gather. You watch through a slit in the stone.

Steel flashes in the morning sun—swords, spears, something else. Not just villagers… soldiers. And not just soldiers—mages. Their cloaks are stitched with silver sigils. Their staffs crackle faintly in the ash-filled air.

One of them, taller than the rest, steps forward. A silver helm glints beneath his hood. He raises a scroll and speaks.

“The wyrmling has awakened. The Seer’s vision was clear. If it reaches its full power, it will not remember mercy.”

The crowd murmurs. A child—no older than you once were—clutches a ragged doll and stares up at your mountain.

“We strike before sundown,” the armored man continues. “If the creature is still confused, we may yet bind it. If not—”
He draws a blade etched in dragonbone. “We finish what the old ones failed to.”

You feel something deep in your chest—hotter than flame, colder than fear.

They think you're confused. Weak.

And they are not entirely wrong.

But they also don’t know you’re listening.

Do you:

🔥 [Launch a surprise attack before they climb the mountain?]

🗺 [Sneak out the back tunnels to find the Seer they mentioned?]

💭 [Stay hidden and use your dragon senses to listen further—maybe one of them has doubts]
