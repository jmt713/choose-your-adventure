🔥 Ending: The Last Ember

The flames are gone. The valley lies still—carved into charcoal and silence.

You stand amidst the ruin, ash swirling in slow eddies around your talons. Smoke curls from the shattered trees and hollowed homes. The wind does not howl; it mourns.

You look down at your claws, at the blackened earth that still smolders beneath your breath.

Victory.
But it is the kind that hollows, not heals.

The knight is gone. Nothing remains of the village but bones of stone and shadow. They feared the monster you might become… and now they will fear the one you truly are.

The ancient voice in your mind—so loud before—is quiet now. Not absent. Merely watching.
Waiting.

“So you have chosen fire. Now sleep, and let the world remember—or forget—what woke beneath the mountain.”

You turn away from the ruin.
Step by heavy step, you retreat into the cavern where your awakening began.

The sun sets behind you, bleeding through the smoke in streaks of dying gold.

Deep within the mountain, the air grows cool. Silent. Eternal.

You curl your wings around your body, lay your head upon a bed of obsidian and ash, and close your eyes.

The fire within you dims…
but never dies.

Centuries may pass.

Forests may regrow. Villages may rise anew.
Legends may twist your tale into myth.

But somewhere beneath the mountain, the last ember sleeps—
waiting to burn again.
